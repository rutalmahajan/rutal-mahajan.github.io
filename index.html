About

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Utica University. My work connects language technology with high performance computing: privacy-preserving and quantum NLP, explainable AI, and HPC-based agentic workflows. I also build ways to teach parallel computing and AI in core computing courses.

I completed my Ph.D. at SVNIT Surat, India (2022) on computational humor and emotion recognition in a multilingual context, after an M.Tech. there in 2014. I have taught for over a decade in India and the United States. I also hold a Sangeet Visharad in Bharatnatyam dance.

Research interests: natural language processing, low-resource languages, high performance computing, machine learning, responsible AI, and pedagogy in computer science.
